Every goal gets its own streak

What you get

Everything in Win Zero, and more

  • Up to three goals

    Each goal has its own check-in time, reminder, streak, best run, bombs, shields and history. Home shows what's open now and what's up next. Done with a goal? Archive it, and its history waits under Past goals.

  • Check-in days you choose

    Every day, weekdays, or any mix, for each goal. Rest days are just rest: nothing is gained or lost on them.

  • Five arcade games

    Pick your game before every new round. None can be lost: no lives, no timers. Clear the last zero for the big finish.

  • Themes and app icons

    Color themes for your rounds and the zeros on Home, each with an app icon to match.

  • Gentle streak rules

    A Not today usually costs one zero, missed days start free, and shields can save a streak. Rest days never count as misses.

  • Private by design

    No account, analytics or ads. Reminders keep your goals off the lock screen unless you choose, and each goal can have a private lock-screen name.
