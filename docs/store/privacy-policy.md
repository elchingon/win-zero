# Win Zero Privacy Policy

_Effective [EFFECTIVE_DATE]_

Win Zero is a daily check-in app made by E7 Systems. This policy explains what the app does
with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and we never see it.

## What the app stores

To work, Win Zero keeps these on your device:

- The goal you type in, and your check-in and morning reminder times.
- Your answers (Yes, Not today, or missed) for each day, and the streak, best run, weapons,
  bombs and shields that come from them.
- Your settings, such as sound, haptics and reminders.

This information is saved in the app's own storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to
anyone else.

## What we collect

Nothing. Win Zero has no accounts and no servers. It does not include analytics, advertising,
tracking, or crash-reporting tools. We do not collect, sell or share any personal information,
and we don't know who uses the app.

## Notifications

Check-in reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no server sends them. By default a
reminder says "your goal", so your goal stays off the lock screen. If you turn on **Goal in
reminders** in Settings, reminders include your goal's wording. You can turn reminders off at
any time in your phone's settings.

## Backups

If your phone backs up apps (for example with iCloud or Google backup), its backup may include
Win Zero's data. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. We never
receive them.

## The support line

Settings and some screens offer a link to a free support line. Tapping it opens your phone's
dialer. Win Zero doesn't record that you did.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Erase all data** to delete everything Win Zero has stored. Uninstalling the
app also deletes it.

## Children

Win Zero is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
