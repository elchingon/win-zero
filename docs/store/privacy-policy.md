# Win Zero Privacy Policy

_Effective October 21, 2026_

Win Zero and Win Zero Plus are daily check-in apps made by E7 Systems. This policy explains what
both apps, and the website at winzero.app, do with your information. The short version: everything
stays on your device, and we never see it. Where this policy says "the app", it means either one.

## What the app stores

To work, the app keeps these on your device:

- The goal you type in, and your check-in and morning reminder times.
- Your answers (Yes, Not today, or missed) for each day, and the streak, best run, weapons,
  bombs, shields and credits that come from them.
- Your settings, such as sound, haptics and reminders.
- In Win Zero Plus, also: each of your goals (up to 3) and the goals you've archived, with their
  check-in days and any lock-screen names you give them, and the game, theme and app icon you pick.

This information is saved in the app's own storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to
anyone else.

## What we collect

Nothing. Neither app has accounts, and nothing you enter is sent to a server. It does not include
analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting tools. We do not collect, sell or share any
personal information, and we don't know who uses the app.

## The website

You can also play Win Zero in your browser at winzero.app. The website works the same way as the
app:

- Your goal, answers and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your device. They are
  not sent to us, and they don't sync with the app or with other browsers.
- The website has no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't load
  anything from other companies.
- The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and
  basic details of each request (such as the page and your browser type) to deliver the site and
  protect it from abuse, under its own privacy policy. We don't use them to identify you.

## Buying Win Zero Plus

Win Zero Plus is paid for once, when you download it. Apple or Google handles the purchase and your
payment details under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card details. Neither app has
in-app purchases.

## Notifications

Check-in reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no server sends them. By default a
reminder says "your goal", so your goal stays off the lock screen. If you turn on **Goal in
reminders** in Settings, reminders include your goal's wording. You can turn reminders off at
any time in your phone's settings.

## Moving to Win Zero Plus

Win Zero Plus is our separate, paid app. If you install it, Win Zero can move your goals,
history, rewards, credits and settings into it. The move happens on your phone; nothing is sent
to us. On iPhone the data passes through a folder that only the two apps can open, and on Android
it is handed straight to Win Zero Plus. Once Win Zero Plus has it, Win Zero stops its own
reminders. Win Zero Plus keeps everything on your phone in the same way as Win Zero.

## Backups

If your phone backs up apps (for example with iCloud or Google backup), its backup may include
the app's data. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. We never
receive them.

## The support line

Settings and some screens offer a link to a free support line. Tapping it opens your phone's
dialer. The app doesn't record that you did.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Erase all data** to delete everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the
app also deletes it. On the website, **Settings → Erase all data** works the same way, and so does
clearing your browser's data for winzero.app.

## Children

Neither app is directed to children under 13, and neither collects information from anyone.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
