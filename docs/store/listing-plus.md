# Store listing copy: Win Zero Plus

Copy for Win Zero Plus's own records in App Store Connect and the Google Play Console. Win Zero's
is in `listing.md`. Every field with a limit lists its character count, checked against that limit.
The tone matches the app: supportive and plain. No medical claims, and no promises about outcomes.

The description opens with Plus's own benefits, then explains its relationship to the free app.
App Store guideline 4.3(a) prohibits multiple Bundle IDs of the same app, and reviewers need a
clear account of the functional differences (`answers-plus.md` has the review notes).
Different copy does not guarantee acceptance of separate Free and Plus apps; see
[review.md](review.md). Plus shares Win Zero's privacy policy and support pages, which cover both apps:
- Privacy policy: `https://winzero.app/privacy`
- Support: `https://winzero.app/support`

### Description (4000 max): 3078
```
Track up to three habits on your own schedule, and turn each Yes into an arcade round. Win Zero Plus is a habit tracker with daily reminders, five games, and themes to earn as your best streak grows.

Read more, take a walk, or work toward another goal you choose. Each goal has its own check-ins and progress. Your answer counts right away; play the round whenever you like.

UP TO THREE GOALS
• Track up to three goals, each with its own check-in time, reminder, streak, best run, bombs, shields and history.
• Home shows what's open now and what's up next, with each goal's stats and calendar a tap away.
• Done with a goal? Archive it. Its history waits under Past goals.

CHECK-IN DAYS
• Pick the days each goal checks in: every day, weekdays, or any mix.
• Rest days are just rest. Nothing is gained or lost on them.

FIVE ARCADE GAMES
• Play each round as Shooter, Catch, Orbit, Slice or Breaker, and pick your game before every new round.
• Each game has seven power-up tiers, starting with one available immediately. The rest unlock as your best run grows, and stay unlocked.
• No game can be lost: no lives, no timers. Clear the last zero for the big finish.

THEMES AND APP ICONS
• Seven color themes for your rounds and the zeros on Home. Classic, Neon Arcade, Mono CRT and Pastel are yours from the start; Ember, Aurora and Gold Year are earned at a best run of 30, 180 and 365 days.
• An app icon to match each theme.

GENTLE STREAK RULES
• Every Yes adds one to that goal's streak. Not today usually subtracts one; on every seventh slip without a Yes, a shield protects the remaining streak or it resets.
• The first 7 missed check-ins in a row cost nothing. Later misses count as slips. Rest days never count as misses.
• Earn bombs, shields and credits through check-ins. Credits play bonus rounds or let you try a locked power-up for one round; they are never sold.

MOVING FROM WIN ZERO
• Win Zero is our separate, free app for one daily goal and the Shooter game. Already using it? Bring your goal, history, streak, rewards and credits over on the same phone.

PAY ONCE
• One purchase. No in-app purchases, no ads, no subscription. All five games are available from the start; some power-ups, themes and icons are earned through check-ins.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account or sign-in. Check in and play offline.
• Goals and check-ins are stored on your phone, with no app account or cloud sync. Win Zero Plus has no analytics or advertising SDKs.
• Reminders keep your goals off the lock screen unless you choose to show them, and each goal can have a private lock-screen name.
• Erase saved progress from Settings. Your phone's system backups are managed separately.

BUILT WITH CARE
If your goals are hard ones, Win Zero Plus is meant to be on your side. The copy is never shaming, a reset is framed as a fresh start, and Settings includes a link to a free, confidential support line (US).

Win Zero Plus works with VoiceOver, larger text sizes and Reduce Motion. Settings › Accessibility adds Reduce effects, Auto-fire, a Finish round button and Bold text.
```

## Accessibility claims
Both descriptions name VoiceOver or TalkBack, larger text and reduced motion, as Win Zero's do. Keep
those lines only once QA rows 7–9, 24, 25 and 56–64 in `docs/RELEASE.md` pass on a Plus build: each
game has to work with a screen reader (Finish round), at the largest text, and with less motion.


## Before pasting

Keywords are relevant search hypotheses, not measured search-volume or ranking claims. Do not
repeat title/subtitle words or add competitor names. Only choose Play tags offered in the console.
For a first release, leave What's New empty if permitted; later release notes must describe the
changes in the submitted build. Confirm the Move section against working store builds of both apps.
