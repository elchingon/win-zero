# Win Zero Support

Need a hand? Here's how Win Zero and Win Zero Plus work, and fixes for the most common questions. If
you're still stuck, email e7urgent@gmail.com.

**If you need support with drinking, drugs or your mental health:** the SAMHSA National Helpline
is free, confidential and open 24/7 in the US: **1-800-662-4357**. The number is also under
**Settings → Need support?** in the app.

## How check-ins work

- Every day at your check-in time (8:00 PM unless you change it), Win Zero asks whether you stuck
  to your goal today.
- If you haven't answered by your reminder time, one more reminder comes (9:00 AM the next
  morning unless you change it). It can be any time of day: set it later than your check-in time,
  for example after a night shift, and it comes later that same day. Set it to your check-in time
  and you get one notification a day.
- There's no rush: you can answer until midnight the next day. Use the notification's buttons, or
  open the app. If yesterday's check-in is still open when today's arrives, the app asks about
  yesterday first.
- A **Yes** from the notification opens the app with your round ready. Play it whenever you like.

## Yes, Not today, and missed days

- **Yes** adds a zero to your streak, and you play a round with that many zeros.
- **Not today** costs one zero, not your whole run. Tapped it by mistake? **Undo Not today** shows
  for a few seconds afterwards.
- **Missed days:** the first 7 in a row cost nothing. After that, each missed day costs a zero.
- **The seventh slip:** after 7 slips since your last Yes, your streak starts over, unless a shield
  steps in to save it.
- Your best run, your weapons and your bombs are never taken away.

## Weapons, bombs and shields

- **Weapons** unlock as your best run grows: Twin Barrel at 3 days, Scatter Gun at 7, Rocket at
  14, Chain Zapper at 30, Rail Laser at 60 and Zero Nuke at 100. Tap the weapon button during a
  round to switch.
- **Bombs:** you earn one each time your streak reaches a multiple of 5. Use them in a round to
  clear a crowd of zeros.
- **Shields:** you earn one each time your streak reaches a multiple of 7, and can hold up to 3.

## Game and Weapon credits

- **A Game credit** plays a bonus round with as many zeros as you like, up to 100,000. Tap **Play a
  bonus round** on Home. A bonus round is just for fun: it doesn't change your streak.
- **A Weapon credit** lends you a weapon you haven't unlocked yet, for one round. You're asked
  before a round whether to use one.
- You earn them by checking in. Tap the "i" beside each count on Home to see every way.

## Reminders don't arrive

- **Check that notifications are on** for Win Zero, in your phone's settings. Settings in Win Zero
  shows whether they're on, and links you there.
- **Check Focus or Do Not Disturb.** These can hide reminders.
- **On Android**, reminders can arrive a few minutes late. Android groups alarms to save battery.
  If they're much later than that, turn off battery optimization for Win Zero.
- **After a long break**, daily reminders pause. Win Zero sends one gentle reminder a week instead
  (you can turn that off in Settings). Answering any check-in turns daily reminders back on.

## Changing your goal or times

Open **Settings** to change the goal's wording or your check-in and reminder times. Your streak and
history stay the same. **Settings → How it works** explains the rules in plain words.

## Accessibility

Win Zero works with VoiceOver and TalkBack. The round is visual, so with a screen reader on, the
round shows a **Finish round** button (on iPhone, a two-finger double-tap does the same). The app
follows your phone's text size and Reduce Motion settings.

**Settings → Accessibility** has four more switches, all off until you turn them on:

- **Reduce effects:** less shake, much dimmer flashes and fewer particles, and a still background,
  without changing your whole phone.
- **Auto-fire:** after your first touch, firing keeps going, so you only need to aim.
- **Finish round button:** shows **Finish round** in every round, screen reader or not.
- **Bold text:** heavier text everywhere, from the next time you open the app.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, and neither app collects any data. Read the
[privacy policy](https://winzero.app/privacy).

## Playing on the web

You can also play Win Zero in your browser at [winzero.app/play](https://winzero.app/play).

- **Your progress stays in that browser.** It doesn't sync with the app or with other browsers.
- **The website can't send reminders.** Open it at your check-in time, or use the app for
  reminders.
- **Clearing your browser's data for winzero.app erases your progress.** Safari can also clear it
  after 7 days without a visit. Adding Win Zero to your Home Screen keeps it.
- **Demo mode** (under Demo controls on Home) lets you move the clock, jump to any day and play
  practice rounds on a copy of your progress. Your real streak isn't touched, and leaving demo
  mode takes you back to it.

## Starting over

**Settings → Erase all data** deletes your goals, history and rewards from your phone. This can't be
undone.

## Win Zero Plus

Win Zero Plus is our separate, paid app: everything in Win Zero, plus more goals, check-in days, four
more games, themes and app icons. It's a one-time purchase, with no in-app purchases, ads or
account. Win Zero stays free and complete.

### Goals and check-in days

- **Up to 3 goals.** Each has its own wording, check-in and reminder times, streak, best run,
  bombs, shields, history and round. Add one from **Settings → Goals → Add a goal**, or **Add
  goal** on Home.
- **Shared rewards.** Weapons unlock from the best run any of your goals has reached, and credits go
  in one wallet. Adding or archiving a goal never takes a weapon away.
- **Check-in days.** Each goal checks in on the days you pick: every day, weekdays, or any mix.
  Rest days have no check-in, so nothing is gained or lost on them. A goal's last check-in before a
  rest day stays open until midnight the next day, as always.
- **Changing the days** starts from the next check-in day that hasn't opened yet. Days already
  answered, or open now, stay as they are.
- For a goal that doesn't check in every day, the free misses are counted in check-ins: the first
  7 missed check-ins in a row cost nothing.
- **Reminders** come for each goal, with their own Yes and Not today. With **Goal in reminders**
  off, a reminder calls a goal by its **Lock-screen name** if you set one, or by its place ("your
  second goal").

### Archive, Past goals and Delete for good

- To make room, open a goal from **Settings** and tap **Archive this goal**. Its reminders stop and
  its slot frees up. With one goal, you change its wording instead.
- Archived goals wait under **Settings → Past goals**, read-only, with their history.
- **Delete for good** is only there, in Past goals. It asks first, and a deleted goal can't come
  back. Weapons and themes it unlocked stay yours.

### Games

- Every round can be played in five games: **Shooter** (Win Zero's), **Catch**, **Orbit**, **Slice**
  and **Breaker**. Each has its own power-ups, which unlock with your best run, like weapons.
- Before each new round, **Pick your game** asks which one to play it in. Tapping outside the list
  plays your last pick, and a replay keeps the game you played.
- **Settings → Games** sets your go-to game. Read to the end of that section: it's worth it.

### Themes

- **Settings → Themes** recolors your rounds and the zeros drifting on Home. Classic, Neon Arcade,
  Mono CRT and Pastel come with Plus.
- Ember opens at a best streak of 30, Aurora at 180 and Gold Year at 365. Once open, a theme is
  yours for good.

### App icon

- **Settings → App icon** offers the Plus icon and one for each theme. A theme's icon opens with the
  theme.
- **On iPhone**, the phone always says "You have changed the icon for Win Zero Plus." That's iOS,
  and nothing's wrong.
- **On Android**, the app asks first, and may close to make the change if you opened it from its
  home-screen icon. Open it again from the new icon. Everything is as you left it.

### Moving from Win Zero

Already using Win Zero? You can bring everything over: goals, every day of history, streaks,
bombs, shields, weapons, credits and settings.

1. Install Win Zero Plus.
2. In Win Zero, open **Settings → About → Move to Win Zero Plus**, and tap **Move to Win Zero
   Plus**.
3. Win Zero Plus opens and shows what's arriving. Nothing changes until you tap **Bring it over**.

Good to know:

- **It happens on your phone.** Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
- **Win Zero goes quiet.** Once Plus has your goals, Win Zero stops its reminders, so you don't get
  two. Its Home says "Your goals now live in Win Zero Plus."
- **Changed your mind?** Tap **Keep using Win Zero here**. Win Zero's reminders come back, with your
  progress as it was on the move day.
- **"Did your move finish?"** If Win Zero doesn't hear back from Plus within about 10 minutes, it
  asks. Tap **Try again** to open Plus on the same move, or **Yes, stop reminders here** if your goals
  arrived. A move delivered twice adds its credits once.
- **Plus already has 3 goals?** It asks you to make room first: archive one, then move again.
- **Plus already has that goal?** It asks which copy to keep, suggesting the one answered most
  recently.
- **Win Zero Plus isn't installed yet?** The move opens its store page, and nothing changes in Win
  Zero.
