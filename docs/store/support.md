# Win Zero Support

Need a hand? Here's how Win Zero works, and fixes for the most common questions. If you're still
stuck, email e7urgent@gmail.com.

**If you need support with drinking, drugs or your mental health:** the SAMHSA National Helpline
is free, confidential and open 24/7 in the US: **1-800-662-4357**. The number is also under
**Settings → Need support?** in the app.

## How check-ins work

- Each evening at your check-in time (8:00 PM unless you change it), Win Zero asks whether you
  stuck to your goal today.
- If you haven't answered by your reminder time, one more reminder comes (9:00 AM the next
  morning unless you change it). It can be any time of day: set it later than your check-in time,
  for example after a night shift, and it comes later that same day. Set it to your check-in time
  and you get one notification a day.
- There's no rush: you can answer until midnight the next day. Use the notification's buttons, or
  open the app. If yesterday's check-in is still open when today's arrives, the app asks about
  yesterday first.
- A **Yes** from the notification opens the app with your round ready. Play it whenever you like.

## Yes, Not today, and missed days

- **Yes** adds a zero to your streak, and you play a round with that many zeros.
- **Not today** costs one zero, not your whole run.
- **Missed days:** the first 7 in a row cost nothing. After that, each missed day costs a zero.
- **The seventh slip:** after 7 slips since your last Yes, your streak starts over, unless a shield
  steps in to save it.
- Your best run, your weapons and your bombs are never taken away.

## Weapons, bombs and shields

- **Weapons** unlock as your best run grows: Twin Barrel at 3 days, Scatter Gun at 7, Rocket at
  14, Chain Zapper at 30, Rail Laser at 60 and Zero Nuke at 100. Tap the weapon button during a
  round to switch.
- **Bombs:** you earn one each time your streak reaches a multiple of 5. Use them in a round to
  clear a crowd of zeros.
- **Shields:** you earn one each time your streak reaches a multiple of 7, and can hold up to 3.

## Reminders don't arrive

- **Check that notifications are on** for Win Zero, in your phone's settings. Settings in Win Zero
  shows whether they're on, and links you there.
- **Check Focus or Do Not Disturb.** These can hide reminders.
- **On Android**, reminders can arrive a few minutes late. Android groups alarms to save battery.
  If they're much later than that, turn off battery optimization for Win Zero.
- **After a long break**, daily reminders pause. Win Zero sends one gentle reminder a week instead
  (you can turn that off in Settings). Answering any check-in turns daily reminders back on.

## Changing your goal or times

Open **Settings** to change the goal's wording or your check-in and morning times. Your streak and
history stay the same.

## Accessibility

Win Zero works with VoiceOver and TalkBack. The round is visual, so with a screen reader on, the
round shows a **Finish round** button (on iPhone, a two-finger double-tap does the same). The app
follows your phone's text size and Reduce Motion settings.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, and Win Zero collects no data. Read the
[privacy policy]([PRIVACY_URL]).

## Starting over

**Settings → Erase all data** deletes your goal, history and rewards from your phone. This can't be
undone.
